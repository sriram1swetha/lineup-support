🎮 What is LineUp?

LineUp is a precision drawing game for iOS. Each level presents a hidden path — straight lines, sweeping curves, intricate mazes, everyday objects, nature scenes, symbols, or expressive faces — and your job is to trace it as closely and quickly as possible. The better your accuracy and speed, the higher your score and the more coins you earn.

👤 Account & Sign-In

You can start playing immediately as a Guest, or sign in with Apple ID for cross-device leaderboard sync. Here's what each option means and how to manage your account.

Choosing how to play

🎭

Guest Play

Pick a name and play instantly — no sign-in required. Your scores and coins are stored locally on your device. If you delete and reinstall the app, local data cannot be recovered.

Sign in with Apple

Link your Apple ID for private, secure authentication. Your leaderboard entry is tied to your Apple ID so your rank follows you across devices. Your email is never shared.

Upgrading from Guest to Apple ID: If you first played as a Guest and later sign in with Apple, your existing scores are automatically migrated to your Apple ID — nothing is lost.

Sign Out

🔓

What happens when you sign out

Signing out returns you to the Welcome screen. Your Apple ID is unlinked, but all local scores, coins, and progress remain on the device. When you sign back in with the same Apple ID, your leaderboard entries are still associated with your account in the cloud.

Deactivate Account (90-day hold)

⏸️

Temporary deactivation — 90-day hold

Deactivating puts your account on hold for up to 90 days. During this period your leaderboard presence is suspended, but your data is preserved. You can reactivate at any time within the 90-day window and everything comes back exactly as you left it.

If the 90-day window expires without reactivation, the account is permanently deleted automatically.

Delete Account (permanent)

🗑️

Permanent deletion — cannot be undone

Choosing Delete Account immediately and permanently removes:

  • Your player profile and display name
  • All leaderboard scores in the cloud
  • All local scores, coin balances, and saved paths
  • Your Apple ID link (if applicable)

A new Guest session is created automatically. This action cannot be reversed — there is no grace period.

Important: Account deletion satisfies Apple's App Store requirement for user-initiated data removal. All data — both on-device and in iCloud — is erased immediately upon confirmation.

🕹️ How to Play

  1. Choose a Level — Pick any level type from the home screen. Early games unlock automatically; later ones unlock as you complete prior games.
  2. Study the guide — A dashed outline shows the path you need to trace. Take a moment to plan your stroke.
  3. Trace it — Touch and drag to follow the path. Lift your finger at each segment endpoint, then tap to start the next segment.
  4. Score & coins — Your score is based on accuracy plus a speed bonus. Coins are awarded when you beat your previous best.
  5. Use the Ghost — On a replay, toggle Ghost in the toolbar to see your last attempt as a faint overlay. Use it to improve segment by segment.
  6. Leaderboard — Scores sync automatically. Visit the leaderboard to see your rank among all players.
Tip: Accuracy matters more than speed for your first play. Once you nail the path, push your speed on subsequent attempts to maximise the time bonus.

🪙 Coins & Rewards

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my scores disappear after reinstalling?

Scores and progress are stored locally on your device. A reinstall wipes local storage. To protect your leaderboard position across installs, sign in with Apple ID — your cloud scores remain safe even if you reinstall.

Can I play on multiple devices with the same account?

Yes — sign in with the same Apple ID on each device. Your leaderboard scores sync through iCloud. Local progress (coins, path history) is device-specific and does not sync between devices.

Why aren't my coins increasing even though I played?

Coins are only awarded when you beat your previous best coin total for that game. If your new score earns the same or fewer coins than your record, no payout occurs. Try improving your accuracy or speed.

How do I unlock the next game in a level?

Complete the previous game in the level to unlock the next one. A game is considered complete once you reach the score screen after tracing all segments.

What is the Ghost path?

The Ghost is a faint, dashed overlay of your most recent attempt at a game. It appears automatically when you replay. Each segment of the ghost is tinted to reflect how well you scored on it previously — so you can immediately see which segments need work. Tap the Ghost button in the top toolbar to toggle it on or off.

What does Deactivate Account do vs Delete Account?

Deactivate puts your account on a 90-day hold — all your data is preserved and you can reactivate any time within that window. Your leaderboard entry is hidden while deactivated.

Delete is immediate and permanent. All data — local and cloud — is erased and cannot be recovered. A new Guest session is started automatically.

I forgot to sign in with Apple before reinstalling — are my scores gone?

Your local scores are gone after a reinstall if you were a Guest. However, if you previously uploaded scores to the leaderboard as a Guest, those cloud entries persist. Signing in with Apple ID on the new install will give you a fresh local device but your cloud rank remains visible.

How is my score calculated?

Each segment is scored on accuracy (how closely you traced the path) plus a time bonus for completing it quickly. Scores range from 1–100 per segment. Your game total is the sum of all segment scores.

🔒 Privacy & Data

LineUp is designed to collect only what is absolutely necessary to run the leaderboard and nothing more.

📋

What we collect

Display name, optional Apple ID (hashed), and game scores — only when you choose to participate in the leaderboard.

🚫

What we never collect

No location data, no advertising IDs, no contacts, no camera access, no behavioural tracking.

☁️

Where data is stored

Leaderboard data is stored in Apple CloudKit (your iCloud). We have no separate backend servers.

🗑️

Your right to delete

Use Delete Account inside the app to permanently remove all your data from every device and iCloud instantly.

Data Deletion

To delete all your data, open LineUp → Settings → Account → Delete Account. This is immediate and permanent. Alternatively, email us at photerrificshots@gmail.com and we will process your request within 30 days.
